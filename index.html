Frequently asked questions about Shapurji Clinic

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking on WhatsApp or phone gets you a confirmed slot and shorter wait.

What are the consultation fees?

Fees vary by doctor. Call or WhatsApp the front desk for the current fee of the doctor you want to see.

Do you do blood tests and home collection?

Yes — pathology tests and full-body packages (from ₹999) are done here, and home sample collection is available. Call or WhatsApp to schedule a pickup.

Is there a doctor available on Sunday in New Town?

Yes. Cardiologist Dr. Krishanko Das sees patients on Sunday at 10 AM and nephrologist Dr. Taniya Bhuiya on Sunday at 11 AM. Please call to confirm before visiting.

Do you have evening doctors?

Yes — most of our specialists sit in the evening (5 PM – 8 PM), including paediatrics, gynaecology, dermatology, cardiology and pain care. See the doctor list for exact days.

Where can I get a sugar (diabetes) test near Shapoorji?

Right here. Fasting sugar and HbA1c are available on their own or in our health packages (from ₹999), and our general physicians help manage diabetes, BP and thyroid.

Is there a lady doctor or gynaecologist near me in New Town?

Yes — four gynaecologists consult here: Dr. Kavita Mandal, Dr. Debasmita Dwibedi, Dr. Shivanjali Nayak and Dr. Lipika Das (Mukhopadhyay).

How do I reach Shapurji Clinic?

We're on the 1st floor of DLF Heights Plaza (Shop 117), Action Area III, New Town – near the Shapoorji bus stop and Sukhobrishti complex. Tap Directions for Google Maps.

Which payment modes are accepted?

Cash, UPI and cards are commonly accepted — please confirm at the counter.

Is parking available?

Please check with the clinic when you book.
